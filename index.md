This policy covers Haltera for Android, a strength-training log. It explains what happens to your information when
you use it. In short: everything stays on your phone.

## What Haltera stores

Haltera keeps the following on your device only:

- the workouts you log (date, exercises, weights, reps, duration);
- your plans and any exercises you create;
- your settings (weight unit, rest time, warm-up sets, reminder times).

There is no account and no sign-up. Haltera asks for no name, email address or other personal details.

## What Haltera collects or shares

Nothing. The Android app does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your information anywhere.
Haltera contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and we never sell or share your data.

## Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Haltera schedules them on your device. They are not sent from a server.

## Backups

Your phone's own backup service (Android Backup), if you have it switched on, may include Haltera's data
in your personal backup. That backup is handled by Google under its terms; we cannot see it.

## Deleting your data

Settings > Erase all workouts removes your workout history. Uninstalling Haltera removes all of its data from your
device.

## Children

Haltera is not directed at children under 13.
